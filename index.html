04 · DEMOCRACY IN THE REAL WORLD

The Gyanesh Kumar
controversy

CURRENT CASE STUDY
Updated Oct. 2026

Why include a political controversy in a voter-awareness project? Because democratic participation also means understanding how election processes are debated, checking claims carefully, and distinguishing allegations from established facts.

01
SIR & electoral rolls

The Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision (SIR) has become a major point of public and political debate over voter-list revisions.

02
Questions & objections

Reporting in September 2026 described objections raised by Election Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi concerning aspects of the SIR process. The ECI said its decisions were ultimately unanimous.

03
Political protests

Opposition parties have demanded Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar's resignation and protested over alleged irregularities. The ECI and BJP have rejected allegations of wrongdoing.

04
Why voters should care

Whatever one's political view, election-related claims should be checked against reliable evidence. Electoral-roll accuracy and public trust are important to a functioning democracy.

WHAT IS VERIFIED?

A public dispute is happening.

The controversy and protests are documented. Specific allegations about wrongdoing remain contested, so this site does not present them as proven facts.

THE LESSON

Check. Question. Verify.

Responsible citizenship means examining evidence and hearing competing claims rather than forwarding the loudest version.
